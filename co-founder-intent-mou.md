# MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
## Co-Founder Intent Agreement

*This Memorandum of Understanding is entered into as of [DATE] between:*

**Party A:** [Director Full Name]
**Party B:** [Your Full Name]

---

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement records the mutual understanding between the Parties regarding the potential future involvement of Party B as a co-founder in [Company/Project Name] ("the Company"). It reflects the genuine intent of both Parties and serves as a shared reference point for the relationship going forward.

---

## 2. Current Status of Party B

Party B is currently not a co-founder or employee of the Company and may provide advisory and technical support on a voluntary basis during this period.

Party B's participation during this period is non-exclusive. Party B may pursue, continue, or take on any professional commitments, opportunities, or ventures during this period. Any non-compete obligation shall only apply upon Party B officially joining the Company as a co-founder and shall be addressed in a separate agreement at that time.

---

## 3. Trigger Events — Decision Point

Upon the occurrence of any one of the following trigger events ("Trigger"), either Party may initiate a good-faith discussion about Party B joining as co-founder:

- **Revenue milestone**: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) reaches $________ *(example: $10,000 MRR)*
- **Customer milestone**: ________ paying customers acquired *(example: 10 enterprise customers)*
- **Funding milestone**: Funding round of $________ or more closed *(example: $250,000 seed round)*
- **Time milestone**: ________ months elapsed from the date of this Agreement *(example: 18 months)*

From the date of initiation, both Parties agree to reach a mutual decision within **30 days**. Both Parties may mutually agree in writing to extend this window once for an additional 30 days. If no agreement is reached within the window, the discussion concludes without obligation on either Party. Both Parties may mutually agree in writing to reset or revise milestones and timelines at any time.

---

## 4. Option to Join Early

Party B may elect to join the Company as a co-founder at any time before a Trigger event, entirely at Party B's discretion, on the terms outlined in Section 5.

---

## 5. Co-Founder Terms Upon Joining

If Party B elects to join the Company, the following terms shall apply:

- **Role**: Co-Founder, [Title — e.g., CTO / CPO]
- **Equity**: ______% of the Company *(to be finalized in a formal shareholders agreement at time of joining)*
- **Vesting schedule**: 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, commencing from Party B's official join date
- **Salary**: To be agreed at time of joining based on Company financial position

All equity and compensation terms shall be formalized in a separate legally binding shareholders agreement upon joining.

---

## 6. Advisory Equity

In recognition of advisory and technical contributions made by Party B prior to formally joining the Company, advisory equity may be granted based on the following contribution tiers:

- **Tier 1** — Occasional guidance, feedback, strategic input: **0.5%**
- **Tier 2** — Regular involvement, prototyping, research, strategy sessions: **1–2%**
- **Tier 3** — Substantial code contributions, product development, architecture design: **2–4%**

Both Parties will agree in writing which tier applies, reviewed every 6 months from the date of this Agreement. Advisory equity vests monthly over 2 years from the date of grant, with no cliff. Advisory equity is separate from co-founder equity outlined in Section 5.

---

## 7. If Party B Elects Not to Join

If both Parties conclude that Party B will not be joining following a Trigger event discussion, or if no decision window agreement is reached:

- This Agreement becomes void
- Party B retains no claim to co-founder equity
- Party B incurs no financial penalty or legal obligation of any kind
- Any advisory equity previously agreed and documented in writing remains unaffected

---

## 8. Confidentiality

Both Parties agree to treat information shared in confidence as confidential and not to disclose it to third parties without prior written consent. This obligation survives the end of this Agreement.

---

## 9. Dispute Resolution

Both Parties agree this document reflects their genuine mutual intent. In the event of any dispute, both Parties commit to first attempting resolution through good-faith discussion, followed by mediation if needed. This document may be referenced as evidence of the Parties' mutual intent in any such proceeding.

---

## 10. Amendments

Any changes to this Agreement must be made in writing and acknowledged by both Parties.

---
